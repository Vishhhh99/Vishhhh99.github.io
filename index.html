How I would map and improve a business process. This is a worked example for business and technology analyst roles.

Illustrative case study

Faster, clearer expense reimbursements

The problem

In a mid-sized company, employees submit expense claims by emailing a spreadsheet and receipts. Claims stall with managers, finance re-types the data, and nobody can see where a claim is.

Who is affected

  • Employees, who wait without any status.
  • Managers, who approve by email.
  • Finance, who spend time on re-entry and follow-ups.

Requirements I would write

  • As an employee, I can submit a claim with receipts in one form, so nothing gets lost.
  • As a manager, I get reminders for pending claims, so approvals do not stall.
  • As finance, I see policy exceptions flagged, so I review fewer claims by hand.
  • As an employee, I can see my claim's status, so I do not need to chase anyone.
    Cycle timeDays from submission to payment
    First-time-right rateShare of claims that are not returned for missing information
    Approval waitTime a claim waits with a manager
    Finance effortHands-on time per claim

    The scenario and process steps are written by me to practise business process modeling. This is not client work and uses no real company data, so the metrics above are what I would measure, not results.
